loan officer or translated into Kikuyu.

One question per scripted answer in the recording worksheet, numbered to match
sentence_no in data/manifest.csv and grouped by its domains. Questions are
worded so they do not suggest the answer. Wording is taken from Table 3.1
where a sample question fits: 6, 8, 11, 12, 16, 17, 19, 23, 25, 26, 28, 30,
36, 37. Table 3.1's "Character & Attitude" domain has no recorded answers;

After editing, regenerate the API's copy:
    python scripts/build_questions.py

## Household (1-5)
1. What is your name, and where do you live?
2. How old are you?
3. How many people live in your household?
4. How many children do you have, and how many of them are in school?
5. How long have you lived in this village?

## Income (6-10)
6. What are your main sources of income?
7. About how much do you earn in a month?
8. How regular is this income? Does it change with the seasons?
9. Are there months when you earn little or nothing?
10. Does anyone else in your household bring in money?

## Expenditure (11-15)
11. What are your main monthly expenses?
12. Do you have school fees? How much of your money do they take?
13. What do you pay each month for electricity or other utilities?
14. How often do you buy inputs for your farm or animals, such as feed?
15. What happens to your spending when someone in the family falls sick?

## Assets (16-20)
16. Do you own any livestock? How many animals?
17. Do you own land? How much?
18. Is the land registered? Whose name is on the title deed?
19. What equipment do you use in your work?
20. What is your house built of?

## Debt and repayment (21-25)
21. Have you borrowed money in the last year or two? How much?
22. Have you finished repaying that loan?
23. Do you owe money to anyone at the moment?
24. Have you ever been unable to repay a loan?
25. Were you always able to repay on time? If not, what happened?

## SACCO history (26-30)
26. How long have you been a SACCO member?
27. How much do you save, and how often?
28. Have you borrowed from the SACCO before?
29. Have you ever been a guarantor for someone else's loan?
30. Have you borrowed elsewhere, for example a mobile loan?

## Chama participation (31-35)
31. Do you belong to a chama or self-help group?
32. How much does each member contribute, and how often?
33. How does the group share out the contributions?
34. How long have you been in the group?
35. Has the group ever helped you buy something or meet a need?

## Intent and planning (36-40)
36. What will you use this loan for?
37. How will you repay?
38. How long do you expect to take to repay the loan?
39. If your plan does not work out, how would you still repay?
40. How do you keep track of what you earn and spend?
